## Project development

A first-year project of the computer science engineering program, in virtual reality,
using Unity and Unreal Engine.

We compared Unity (with Tridify) and Unreal Engine (with Datasmith) to find the one
that makes back-and-forth between architects and developers easiest.

**My role:** developed the entire Unity solution (changing the materials of building
elements, visualizing the structure, changing the time of day, connecting to virtual
reality, ...)

## Développement du projet

Projet de première année de cycle informatique, en réalité virtuelle, sur Unity et
Unreal Engine.
